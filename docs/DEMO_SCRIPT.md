# AccountableAI demo script — 75 seconds

## 0–10 sec: set the stakes

“Oakline sends Keyway money for repairs. The question is whether the vendors ever got paid.”

Open **Live investigation**. Point out the five-step trail and press **Play 15-day loop**. If the Python feed is not running, use **Watch saved 15-day replay**; it is visibly labeled as a replay and never enables a real action.

## 10–30 sec: let Keyway make a claim

Say: “Keyway says it paid Rio Grande Plumbing, but attaches no bank record or vendor confirmation.”

Point to the payment-trail card: the vendor notice and bank record outrank Keyway’s statement. Open one citation chip.

## 30–45 sec: show the verifier

“The verifier tries to prove our alert wrong. It does not approve an accusation or an uncited claim.”

Point to the verifier result and its citations. If asked, open **Proof & evals**: the live verifier blocks or refutes 8/8 planted bad drafts.

## 45–60 sec: show human control

“The case agent can only propose this $3,140 hold. It cannot send money or decide it.”

Open the evidence pack. Return to the controller decision. “The controller decides; every decision lands in the audit trail.”

## 60–75 sec: prove correction, not stubbornness

Open **Judge Console** and choose the invoice-received correction. “A real changed fact only rechecks its dependents: Sparkline comes off the hold, while the other evidence stays.”

Close: “AccountableAI doesn’t trust a middleman’s statement. It follows each funded dollar to proof.”
